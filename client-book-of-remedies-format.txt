FORMATTING RULES

1. Begin every chapter with: CHAPTER:
2. Add the chapter title using: TITLE:
3. Mark headings within a chapter using: SECTION:
4. Begin every verse with: VERSE:
5. Restart verse numbering from 1 in every chapter.
6. Remove all CHAPTER BREAK text.
7. Keep cautions and editorial notes inside square brackets.
8. Do not change the original wording, spelling, punctuation, or special characters.
9. Do not place more than one verse under the same VERSE number.
10. If a chapter, section, or verse boundary is uncertain, add [REVIEW REQUIRED] instead of guessing.
